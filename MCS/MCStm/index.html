TRACTSS

Treatments you'd Retry, Avoid, or Currently Take due to any Sensory Sensitivities

v26.09.10

About this checklist

TRACTSS records treatments you have tried because of chemical or other sensory sensitivities. For every treatment, indicate whether you would retry it, would avoid it, or currently take or use it. This gives you and your clinicians a compact record of your treatment experience. The treatment list was compiled by an independent toxicologist, from whom references are available: Albert@DonnayDetox.com.

How to complete the TRACTSS checklist

For each treatment, choose one status:

  • N Never / no memory = never tried the treatment, or do not remember its effects
  • R Retry = tried it and would be willing to retry it
  • A Avoid = tried it and want to avoid trying it again
  • C Currently take = currently take or use the treatment

Every treatment starts at N. If you select R or A, you must select one or more reasons why you stopped it before continuing. The dashboard updates automatically. You can stop and come back anytime; your answers are saved in this browser on your device.

Reasons for stopping a treatment

  • eRx End of prescription
  • $$$ Expense
  • MDa Medical advice (e.g., contraindicated for any reason)
  • NLN No Longer Needed
  • ROT Replace with Other Treatment(s)
  • sFx Side effects

Important: TRACTSS records your experience and preferences; it does not determine whether a treatment is safe or effective. Do not start, stop, avoid, or retry a treatment based on this checklist alone. Discuss treatment changes with a qualified clinician.

Privacy & your data

Your answers are stored only in this web browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to any server. Anyone who uses this browser profile can see your answers, so on a shared or public computer use Clear answers when you finish. To move your report to another device, use your browser's Print → Save as PDF.

Your information

Enter your name so your clinician knows whose report this is — or, if you prefer to stay anonymous, a private 3-word codename up to 12 letters (e.g. redbikedog). Your choice.

Fills in automatically when you enter your first treatment; you can change it.
