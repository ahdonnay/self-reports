Disorders Associated with Chemical Sensitivity in Some

About this checklist

The DACSS is a checklist of medical disorders associated with Chemical Sensitivity in some people. Not everyone with these disorders has MCS, but they are more likely to have MCS, and people with MCS are more likely to have these disorders. The DACSS is designed for people to record which of these disorders they have had in the past, always had, or have currently but not always, so they and their clinicians can see where their illness is coming from in terms of what disorders are involved. (The list was compiled by an independent toxicologist, from whom the references are available: Albert@DonnayDetox.com.)

How to complete the DACSS checklist

For each disorder, choose one:

  • N Never = NEVER had disorder, or cannot recall
  • P Past = had disorder in PAST, but not now
  • A Always = ALWAYS has disorder, since childhood
  • C Current — CURRENTLY have disorder, but not always

Every disorder starts at Never. Change only the ones you have experienced. The dashboard updates as you go. You can stop and come back to finish anytime; your answers are saved in this browser on your device. The checklist takes about 20 minutes to complete.

Bottom Line Although all of these disorders are correlated with MCS, the correlation is not always causal. The disorder may be co-factor that contributes to the risk of getting MCS, or it may be a consequence of getting MCS, a co-morbidity, or just a coincidence. The DACSS you had in the past and recovered from are as important for clinicians to consider as those you still have.

Privacy & your data

Your answers are stored only in this web browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to any server. Anyone who uses this browser profile will be able to see your answers, so on a shared or public computer, use Clear answers when you finish. To move your report to another device, use your browser's Print → Save as PDF.

Your information

Enter your name so your clinician knows whose report this is — or, if you prefer to stay anonymous, a private 3-word codename up to 12 letters (e.g. redbikedog). Your choice.

Fills in automatically when the checklist is complete; you can change it.
