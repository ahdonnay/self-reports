BAACSS

Biomarker Abnormalities Associated with Chemical Sensitivity in Some

v26.09.06

About this checklist

The BAACSS is a checklist of biomarker abnormalities associated with chemical sensitivity in some people. Not everyone with these abnormalities has MCS. But statistically significantly more people with MCS have these abnormalities than controls. The BAACSS is designed for people to record their most recent results as High, Low, or in the Control range, so they and their clinicians can see how closely their results match MCS and which tests they have never or not recently had. (The list was compiled by an independent toxicologist, from whom the references are available: Albert@DonnayDetox.com.)

How to complete the BAACSS checklist

For each biomarker, choose one:

  • N Never / no result = never had this test, or do not have access to the result
  • C Control = result in the control or normal range
  • L Low = result below the control range
  • H High / present = result above the control range, or present if normally absent

Every biomarker starts at N. Change N to C, L, or H only when you have the lab or EHR result in hand. You must then enter its date as YYYY, YYYY.MM, or YYYY.MM.DD before continuing. The dashboard updates automatically. You can stop and come back anytime; your answers are saved in this browser on your device.

Bottom Line Although all of these biomarkers are significantly more abnormal in people with MCS than controls, the correlation is not always causal. The biomarker may be a co-factor that contributes to the risk of getting MCS, or it may be a consequence of getting MCS, a co-morbidity, or just a coincidence. BAACSS results that do not match MCS are as important as results that do.

Important: Record the classification shown on your own report and discuss results with a qualified clinician. This checklist does not establish a diagnosis or replace the reference range and interpretation supplied by the testing laboratory.

Privacy & your data

Your answers are stored only in this web browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to any server. Anyone who uses this browser profile can see your answers, so on a shared or public computer use Clear answers when you finish. To move your report to another device, use your browser's Print → Save as PDF.
