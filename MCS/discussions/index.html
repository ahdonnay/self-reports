Privacy and participation

  • Patients and other participants may use anonymous usernames when discussing their own results.
  • You may attach your own report as a PDF only after removing your real name and all other identifying information.
  • Clinicians and researchers are encouraged to use their real names and professional affiliations.
  • Everyone may read, follow, and receive notifications from every discussion. Posting in the Clinician, Researcher, and Survivor conversations is reserved for people who identify themselves in the corresponding role.
  • A participant may identify as a Clinician, Researcher, and/or Survivor and may post in every conversation matching their roles.
  • The boards are public. Do not post another person's identifying or medical information without permission.
  • Discussion does not replace individualized medical advice.
